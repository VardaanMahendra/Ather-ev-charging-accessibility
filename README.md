# Ather Energy — Enhancing Charging Accessibility & Customer Experience

**Product Management Capstone | AI-Powered Product Management & Growth Marketing**  
**Program:** IIM Rohtak × Masai School  
**Project type:** Team capstone | Product strategy, customer pain-point analysis, and interactive prototyping

## Overview

This capstone explores how Ather Energy could strengthen the Ather Grid charging ecosystem by reducing charging uncertainty, improving route planning, and making public charging more predictable for riders.

**Core problem statement**

> How might Ather Energy use data, intelligent software, and strategic infrastructure expansion to create a more seamless and accessible charging experience, reduce range anxiety, improve charging convenience, and support EV adoption?

This repository contains the project presentation, capstone report, and links to two interactive prototypes.

## Customer and Product Challenges Explored

- Uneven charging coverage and limited access to convenient locations
- Home-charging constraints for apartment residents and customers without dedicated parking
- Uncertainty about charger occupancy and waiting times during peak demand
- Manual charging-stop planning for longer journeys
- Opportunities to improve charging-network utilization and customer experience

These are problem areas explored by the project, not claims that every rider experiences them.

## Proposed Product Strategy

### 1. Charging Infrastructure Analytics Team
Create a cross-functional capability using anonymized charging patterns, geographic data, and demand analysis to identify underserved areas and prioritize potential Neighborhood Charger locations.

### 2. Smart Route Planning — “Plan Charging on the Way”
Propose an Ather App journey-planning feature that recommends charging stops using factors such as battery state of charge, route, traffic, estimated energy use, and charger availability.

[Open the interactive route-planning prototype](https://ai.studio/apps/238fd53c-1aea-4de6-9d6d-1316c4127931)

### 3. Premium Charging Slot Reservation
Explore a paid reservation concept for eligible high-demand Ather Grid locations. The capstone proposes 15-, 20-, and 30-minute slots, a five-minute early/late arrival window, and releasing unused reserved chargers back to public use.

[Open the interactive slot-reservation prototype](https://ather-grid-677520987911.asia-southeast1.run.app)

### 4. Strategic Charging-Network Partnerships
Explore potential partnerships with fuel retailers, commercial properties, residential communities, and other suitable locations to extend charging access.

### 5. Ather Charging+ and Data-Driven Growth
Propose a subscription concept for frequent public-charging users, alongside geospatially targeted outreach to potential charging-site hosts and partners.
